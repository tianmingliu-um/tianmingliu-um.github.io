I am an Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI in Science Fellow at Michigan Institute of Data Science & AI in Society. I obtained my Ph.D. degree in transportation engineering (advised by Dr. Yafeng Yin) and M.S. degree in Industrial and Operations Engineering from University of Michigan in 2025, and have obtained my B.S. degree in control science and engineering from Tsinghua University in 2020. I has been recognized with distinctions including the Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctral Research Fellowship, the Schmidt Science Fellows Global Finalist, and the Richard F. and Eleanor A. Towner Prize for Distinguished Academic Achievement Award. 

My research focuses on the modeling, optimization, and management of intelligent sociotechnical systems, where people, AI, and physical infrastructure interact. I develop data- and AI-driven models of individual behavior and connect them with system-level optimization to design incentives, allocate resources, and coordinate services. Transportation has been the primary application domain of my work to date. Methodologically, I bring together behavioral science, artificial intelligence, and operations research to develop methods that integrate behavioral economics, statistical learning, network modeling, queueing theory, optimization, and generative AI.

I am currently on the academic job market and is seeking a tenure-track assistant professor position related to transportation engineering, industrial and systems engineering and AI & Society.
